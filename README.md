HR_Analytics_Dashboard

Objective
The primary objective of this project is to analyze employee data and identify key factors contributing to employee attrition. This dashboard helps HR teams make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention, satisfaction, and workforce planning.
📌 Overview
The HR Analytics Dashboard provides a comprehensive visualization of employee attrition trends across different dimensions such as age, salary, education, job roles, and years of experience.
It enables stakeholders to:
•	Monitor overall employee count and attrition rate
•	Identify high-risk employee segments
•	Analyze patterns behind employee turnover
•	Improve HR strategies using actionable insights
________________________________________
📊 Key Insights from Dashboard
•	📉 Attrition Rate: 16.1% indicating moderate employee turnover
•	👨‍💼 Total Employees: 1470 with 237 attritions
•	🎓 Education Impact: Highest attrition from Life Sciences (38%)
•	💰 Salary Insight: Majority attrition occurs in salary band up to 5K
•	👩‍🔬 Job Roles: Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives show highest attrition
•	👥 Age Group: Employees aged 26–35 have the highest attrition
•	⏳ Experience: Most employees leave within early years of joining
